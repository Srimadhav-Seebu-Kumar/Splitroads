# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Active traders reviewing their own decision-making after market hours: independent futures/equities day-traders (primary), prop-firm evaluation traders, and trading coaches with client rosters. Context: a tired trader, five minutes after the close, often on a losing day, on a large desktop monitor in a dim room. They live inside screaming terminals all day; this is the quiet room they walk into afterward. Traders are keyboard people; analysis happens on desktop, glances and confirmations on mobile.

The job to be done: see my own behavior (hesitation, conviction, discipline, fear, abandoned intentions) with evidence, understand what it costs me, and change one thing at a time.

## Product Purpose

Split-Roads is a Decision Intelligence Platform: it captures the trades users almost took (intent events), simulates the alternate outcomes (phantoms, as distributions with uncertainty), and builds a behavioral profile (Decision DNA) from the full decision record. Behavior is the primary object; trades are secondary. Success looks like a user saying "it saw something true about me" and measurably improving one behavioral dimension.

This is not a trading platform, broker dashboard, journal, or reporting tool. It is a new category: a living visualization of one person's decision-making.

## Brand Personality

Calm, evidentiary, kind. A sharp, kind coach: second person, present tense, no exclamation marks, no emoji, no shame. Every cost statement arrives with its action. The signature visual is the split road: a path that forks, the taken branch solid, the untaken branch a translucent band whose width is uncertainty. Emotional goals: discovery, curiosity, reflection, self-recognition ("I never realized I do that"), trust.

## Anti-references

- Broker dashboards and legacy finance terminals (dense grids, blinking red/green, urgency by design)
- Trading journals and spreadsheet-shaped analytics (TraderVue, Edgewonk aesthetics)
- Generic SaaS analytics chrome: stat-card grids, hero metrics, identical card walls
- Casino energy: streaks, confetti, gamified engagement, infinite feeds, notification bait
- Profit-green / loss-red as primary semantics (we measure behavior, not P&L)

## Design Principles

1. **One extraordinary centerpiece, instrument-grade everything else.** The River of Forks (the decision timeline rendered as a luminous forking path) carries the wow; all surrounding chrome is calm, precise, keyboard-first. Never layer competing spectacle.
2. **Uncertainty is rendered, not footnoted.** Every estimate is a band, never a point. Translucency and band width are the native language of the interface.
3. **Distributions, not anecdotes.** Aggregates before single cases; evidence gates (n>=20) render as visible accumulation progress, never as withheld-feeling numbers.
4. **The session ends.** Surfaces are designed to close ("Nothing else needs you."), never to maximize time-in-app. Calm is the brand and the moat.
5. **Behavior wears amber and teal, never red and green.** Cost is warm amber, improvement is cool teal, and both always arrive with a way forward.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Amber/teal palette is colorblind-safe via luminance separation and always paired with iconography or labels. All distribution visuals have tabular alternatives. Full keyboard navigation including a command palette. `prefers-reduced-motion` yields a beautiful static alternative for every animated surface, including the WebGL river.
